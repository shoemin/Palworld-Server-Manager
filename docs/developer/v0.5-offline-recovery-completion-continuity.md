# Completion continuity through local offline recovery

Trial reference: [#28](https://github.com/shoemin/Palworld-Server-Manager/issues/28), architecture4b-i step7.

An Owner's approval of a peer credential is independent of this Host's own signing credential. `HostCredentialStateRepository.ReplaceOffline` now preserves an already-valid outgoing completion obligation while installing this Host's fresh recovery credential. It does not approve the fresh credential on another Host, send anything, clear recovery, or manufacture a new approval.

Before the existing offline transaction changes state, a private expectation captures the relevant identity, credential, rotation, principal, grant, default, trust, pairing, candidate, proof, receipt, marker, incarnation and revision tables. An outgoing marker qualifies for continuity only when its peer is currently Active, its approved key equals the current trusted peer key, its current incarnation equals the actual relationship incarnation, and it is uncanceled. Only that marker's `CurrentIncarnation` may carry forward. Its original source/approval incarnation, local fingerprint at approval, confirmation and other history remain unchanged. Canceled, stale, wrong-key, revoked and PeerBound markers never acquire eligibility.

The existing recovery effects remain: select a fresh unused local credential, activate it, abort nonterminal routine rotations, set recovery on Active and PeerBound relationships, and invalidate outstanding replacement records. Candidate invalidation now precedes identity/trust triggers so the canonical transaction timestamp is retained. Recovery0-to1 advances the relationship incarnation; an already-pending recovery retains its incarnation. The expected revision accounts for every existing trust UPDATE, including recovery1-to1. A subsequent [exact-key incoming receipt](v0.5-recovery-completion-receipt.md) can clear recovery and carry the same valid marker through that separate qualified transition. Later revocation or replacement still cancels or supersedes it.

The final audit and expected effects are validated after all writes. A missing or altered recovery audit, changed current credential, mismatched incarnation/marker, altered principal or grant, unexpected rotation state, or any other difference in the captured tables rolls back the complete transaction. The audit preserves the existing reason-specific kind, OfflineRecovery actor, Host identity, offline flag and fixed secret-free summary. No unrelated audit-writing API is changed.

Fault injection against the previously accepted writer demonstrated that an AFTER INSERT trigger could delete the final audit while credential recovery still committed. The regression now requires refusal and complete rollback. The trial ledger records this as an older accepted defect, with its original and corrected evidence, rather than attributing it to the newly added continuity mechanism.

Five new ordinary scenarios cover that regression, continuity through both already-pending and fresh recovery plus a second recovery, canonical candidate timestamps, old-key and mismatched-ack refusal, ineligible markers, later revocation/replacement, and 22 late-effect/audit rollback cases. The focused probe also runs the existing recovery metadata group across all nonterminal rotation states and both recovery reasons. Explicit historical metadata fixtures remain supported; they do not stand in for native TLS or a complete two-Host ceremony.

Existing privileged offline admission, machine exclusivity, secure material providers, public trust publication and retirement coordination are unchanged. This synchronous API adds no cancellation contract. Ordinary authentication/authorization and client boundaries remain unchanged. Future sending must use this Host's actual current credential, never treat the historical fingerprint at approval as a perpetual signing-key requirement, and still authenticate the peer's exact current approved key.

Authenticated completion negotiation/routing, durable sending/confirmation, public commands and full two-Host repair remain subsequent #28 work. Creator permissions and limits are unchanged; accessibility is **WAIVED, never PASS**. Physical-PC/Palworld/parity/Linux/release qualification is not claimed.
