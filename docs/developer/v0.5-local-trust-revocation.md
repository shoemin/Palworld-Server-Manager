# Local transactional trust revocation

Trial reference: [#28](https://github.com/shoemin/Palworld-Server-Manager/issues/28).

`GrantPolicyRepository.RevokeLocalPeerTrust` is a trusted Host persistence seam. Its actor must come from the authenticated local channel. The caller retains the machine lease and supplies the last observed authorization revision and relationship incarnation; neither value grants authority. The exact local `ManageTrustedManagers` capability is required for this Host, with implicit Owner authority. A local administrator may use a correctly delegated grant. `ManagePermissions`, a grant for another Host, OS eligibility and possession of a peer identifier do not substitute.

One immediate SQLite transaction checks current local identity, policy, revision, incarnation and current Host credential. It keeps the `TrustedManagers` row as a `Revoked` tombstone, clears both pins and all pending rotation/recovery flags, and advances the relationship incarnation through the existing never-reused mechanism. Stable `HostId` and historical metadata remain. Outstanding credential-replacement records become permanently invalidated in the same transaction.

The bounded completion marker from [local Owner-approved replacement](v0.5-owner-peer-replacement.md) is also canceled with the canonical transaction timestamp and included in the exact final row validation and audit count. Revocation cannot leave an old approval available for subsequent completion.

The writer selects every Host/server grant whose grantee or issuer is the peer, then follows each type's existing canonical single-parent subtree. Every still-valid row in that union receives an invalidation timestamp. Earlier invalidation timestamps remain untouched, descendants of already-invalidated roots are included, and unrelated same-capability grants retain their exact records. No grant is re-homed, reissued, deleted or created. A local administrator may intentionally revoke the peer from which that administrator's own capability derives: the pre-write authorization and exact expected effects permit that transition, while current local identity must remain valid through commit.

After all mutations and the success audit, the writer verifies the exact expected grants, all trust and pending-replacement rows, revision change, relationship incarnation, current local identity and Host credential. A missing or altered audit or any late inconsistent effect rolls back the whole transaction. The audit identifies the actual local actor separately from the target peer, the prior/current incarnation and invalidation counts. It contains no private key, verifier, fingerprint, request label or arbitrary error text. Pre-write capability denials use the existing canonical denial-only audit.

Repeated revocation of a clean tombstone is a no-op after current identity, permission and stale-request checks. A newly pending replacement or still-uninvalidated grant may be canceled by another explicit revocation request without resurrecting trust or changing the original revocation timestamp. Concurrent requests use the revision/incarnation guards; callers must refresh after a stale refusal. Cancellation before commit rolls back every effect. Completion never invokes transport or waits for peer cooperation, and creates no offline command queue.

## Evidence and remaining integration

`PeerTrustRevocationTests` covers exact forests and unrelated grants, stale fingerprint admission, actual-actor audit, local delegated authority including intentional self-loss, wrong capability/Host/identity refusal, pending replacement cancellation, idempotence, ABA-resistant incarnation checks, cancellation after writes, late state/audit fault rollback, eight concurrent writers, permanent historical invalidation and PeerBound/recovery/staged-rotation cleanup. Its trust and OS identities are explicit SQLite fixtures; transport admission calls the real repository predicate. They do not constitute an installed pairing or revocation RPC ceremony.

This is the local persistence unit of #28. [Authenticated remote administrators and internal dispatch](v0.5-remote-trust-revocation.md) and the [reciprocal receiving transaction](v0.5-reciprocal-unpair-receipt.md) reuse it. The [negotiated receiving RPC](v0.5-reciprocal-unpair-rpc.md) consumes that receipt seam, and the [nonblocking coordinator](v0.5-unpair-coordinator.md) connects prepared delivery to both administrator facades. Public initiating-command and actual remote hang/drop/full re-pair qualification remain subsequent units. Remote administration also requires the originating user's independent local ceiling. A same-HostId pairing against the retained tombstone enters the zero-authority replacement gate with [fresh verified context](v0.5-replacement-candidate-evidence.md); full Owner-approved repair is not yet implemented. The writer invalidates selected replacement requests before changing trust so its exact transaction timestamps remain stable under generic invalidation triggers. It does not install a new endpoint, advertise a capability, alter the six approved creator grants, or qualify physical PCs, Palworld, Windows parity, Linux or a release. Accessibility certification remains **WAIVED, never PASS**.
