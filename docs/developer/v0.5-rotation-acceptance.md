# Fresh multi-peer rotation acceptance

Trial reference: #44r, within #44. The Host's internal acceptance collector connects the [current peer-set snapshot](v0.5-rotation-peer-set.md) to actual [pinned proposal exchanges](v0.5-rotation-proposal-rpc.md). It freezes the supplied Host-owned routes and contacts at most four peers concurrently. Every captured Active peer needs an exact acknowledgement for this proposal, peer UUID and actual current peer key. Pending pairing, recovery, missing routes, contact failure, reconfirmation and outstanding promotion receipts remain explicit blockers. No peer is automatically revoked to make a round succeed.

An acknowledgement must retain strictly more than two minutes of conservative remaining acceptance. This margin is an implementation policy, not a renewal of the peer's original deadline. The exchange subtracts all local elapsed time and cannot revive an expired bound after a clock anomaly. Rechecking reevaluates these bounds and rereads the current proposal, revision and complete non-Revoked peer list. A late peer, activation, promotion, expiry or restored-value trust change invalidates the round. A new collection can retry after the relationship is durably resolved; a failed round's blockers are never silently removed.

Evidence belongs to one collector instance and is held only in immutable process-local containers. Historical acknowledgement timestamps cannot reconstruct it after restart. A peer going offline after acceptance does not alone erase an otherwise valid remaining bound, but a new round must contact that peer again. Cancellation propagates to the bounded contacts and never returns partial success; independently committed peer staging remains available for an idempotent retry with its original deadline.

The assessment reports peer acknowledgement readiness only. It does not move the rotation to ReadyForCutover, change Current, publish a descriptor, restart listeners, retire a key, issue grants or authorize management. The eventual global coordinator must check current Owner authority, the exact proposal, complete peer set and remaining bounds again inside its final transaction under quiescence. This read-only assessment cannot substitute for that transaction.

Eight ordinary scenarios use actual mutual-TLS multi-peer exchanges to cover all-peer acceptance, historical evidence refusal after reopening the collector, offline and missing-route retries, unchanged deadlines, minimum margin and lapse, retained receipts, pending/recovery blockers, durable pending expiry, late membership and restored-value ABA, concurrent abort, actual peer key promotion, decreasing elapsed bounds, instance scope and both pre-call and post-peer-commit cancellation. A deliberately blocked real TLS admission/observation callback verifies that transport disposal waits for Host-state callbacks to finish and prevents new ones from entering; handler cancellation alone did not provide that guarantee. Test setup still uses synthetic Active and prepared New metadata; no complete global switch, process-kill, reboot or physical multi-machine evidence is claimed. Global cutover/retirement, installed service/local initiation and complete #44 qualification remain outstanding.
